Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD IT) is one of the biggest changes to self-employed tax in years. Instead of one annual return habit, many drivers will need **digital records**, **quarterly updates**, and a **final declaration**.

We're based in Belfast and help taxi drivers across Northern Ireland prepare early — not in the week before the first mandated quarter.

## Does MTD apply to you?

It depends on your **qualifying income** (broadly turnover from self-employment and property, before expenses), phased over several years. High-turnover drivers are brought in first. Read the full breakdown: [Making Tax Digital for taxi drivers](/insights/making-tax-digital-for-taxi-drivers).

If you're unsure, [ring us](/contact) with a recent return or a rough turnover figure and we'll place you on the timeline.

## What we help with

- Checking whether and when MTD applies to you
- Getting your [income & expenditure](/services/income-expenditure-records) habits MTD-ready
- Preparing and submitting **Making Tax Digital tax returns** (included on Silver and Gold)
- Keeping your position tidy alongside any remaining [Self Assessment](/services/self-assessment) obligations during the transition
- Avoiding the penalty traps that come with missed quarterly updates

MTD setup is also available **by arrangement** on our à la carte list — useful if you need systems help before you join a monthly plan.

## Why drivers leave this too late

App income, cash jobs, and vehicle costs make quarterly summaries harder if your books are a shoe box. Starting with a simple recording system now is the cheapest way to stay calm when your threshold year arrives.
