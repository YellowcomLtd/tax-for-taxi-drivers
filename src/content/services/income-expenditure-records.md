Good records are the difference between a calm tax year and a scramble in January. For taxi drivers in Belfast and across Northern Ireland, that means capturing mixed income streams and everyday running costs without turning every shift into admin.

We supply an **income & expenditure recording system** on every monthly plan, plus guidance on what to keep - so logging expenses takes minutes.

## What we help you track

- Fare and platform income (including apps such as Uber and Bolt)
- Fuel, servicing, tyres and repairs
- Insurance, licensing and compliance costs
- Phone, card-machine and other running costs
- Home-to-work vs business mileage questions, explained plainly

For a deeper look at allowable costs, read [what taxi drivers can claim as an expense](/insights/what-can-taxi-drivers-claim-as-an-expense).

## How it feeds your tax return

Clean books make [Self Assessment](/services/self-assessment) faster and more accurate. We prepare your income & expenditure account from the same system, then submit the return to HMRC and give you copies.

If you're approaching [Making Tax Digital](/services/making-tax-digital) thresholds, starting tidy digital habits now is far easier than rebuilding a year of paper later. See our guide to [using an income & expenditure system](/insights/using-an-income-expenditure-system).

## Benefits and other uses

The same figures often support [benefit entitlement checks](/services/benefit-claims) and [loss of earnings](/services/loss-of-earnings) calculations. One reliable set of records beats three conflicting stories.
