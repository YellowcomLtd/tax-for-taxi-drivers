Licensing authorities now expect taxi and private-hire drivers to complete an **HMRC tax check** before renewing. You get a code to pass to the council or licensing body — without it, renewal can stall.

Tax for Taxi Drivers completes HMRC tax checks for drivers in Belfast and across Northern Ireland, whether you're on a monthly plan or need a one-off.

## Who needs this

- Hackney carriage and private-hire drivers renewing with a NI or Belfast licensing authority
- Drivers whose renewal window is approaching and who haven't generated a code yet
- Anyone unsure whether their [Self Assessment](/services/self-assessment) registration is in order before they apply

For background, see [what happens if HMRC opens a tax check](/insights/what-happens-if-hmrc-opens-a-tax-check) and [PHV vs hackney carriage tax](/insights/phv-vs-hackney-carriage-tax).

## Pricing

- **£50** one-off (see [other services](/services))

If your licence deadline is close, say so when you [get in touch](/contact) — we'll prioritise the check.

## Not the same as an investigation

A licence tax check is not an [HMRC investigation](/services/hmrc-investigations). If you've received a broader compliance letter, bring that too and we'll separate the two.
