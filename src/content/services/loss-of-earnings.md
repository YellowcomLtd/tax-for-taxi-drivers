Self-employed taxi drivers don't get a neat payslip when something goes wrong. Insurers, solicitors and courts need a **loss of earnings calculation** that reflects real fares, costs, and working patterns - not a guess.

We prepare these calculations for drivers across Belfast and Northern Ireland, using proper income and expenditure evidence.

## Typical uses

- Motor or personal injury claims after time off the road
- Insurance claims needing historic earnings support
- Legal matters where compensation depends on proven income
- Situations that overlap with [benefit claims](/services/benefit-claims) while you're unable to work

## How we calculate it

We work from your [income & expenditure](/services/income-expenditure-records) position - ideally the same figures that sit behind your [Self Assessment](/services/self-assessment). That keeps the number credible and consistent if it's ever questioned.
