Self Assessment is the backbone of tax for self-employed taxi drivers. Whether you drive a Belfast black taxi, a private-hire vehicle, or take jobs through apps, HMRC expects a clear picture of what you earned and what you spent — and a return filed on time.

Tax for Taxi Drivers handles the full process for drivers across Belfast and Northern Ireland: registration, income and expenditure accounts, the tax calculation, and submission to HMRC.

## Who this is for

- Newly licensed drivers who need to [register as self-employed](/insights/registering-as-self-employed-first-time) for the first time
- Drivers who have filed before but want someone else to own the paperwork
- Drivers catching up on late or missing returns
- Anyone moving onto [Making Tax Digital](/services/making-tax-digital) who still needs a clean annual return in the meantime

## What's included

On every monthly plan we:

- Register you for Self Assessment (if you aren't already)
- Register you as our client with HMRC
- Supply an [income & expenditure recording system](/services/income-expenditure-records)
- Prepare and submit your Self Assessment tax return
- Prepare your income & expenditure account

Silver and Gold add extras such as [HMRC tax checks](/services/hmrc-tax-check), pension forecasts, MTD returns, and telephone support — see [full pricing](/services).

## Why local matters

Taxi work in Belfast and across NI has its own patterns: shift work, mixed cash and card, vehicle costs, licensing renewals, and platform income. We build returns around how drivers here actually earn — not a generic self-employment template.

## Deadlines that matter

Online Self Assessment is due by **31 January** following the end of the tax year. Miss it and interest and penalties can stack quickly. If you're unsure where you stand, [ring for a confidential chat](/contact) before the deadline, not after.
