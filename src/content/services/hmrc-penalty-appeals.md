An HMRC penalty letter is stressful - especially when you're working long shifts and the wording feels designed to panic you. Many late-filing and late-payment penalties can be challenged when there's a reasonable excuse, a genuine mistake on HMRC's side, or a better way to get the underlying return and payment sorted first.

We've helped clients appeal more than **£100,000** in HMRC penalties. We take the process on for taxi drivers across Belfast and Northern Ireland.

## Common situations we see

- Missed **31 January** Self Assessment deadline
- Penalties stacking while a return was still outstanding
- Surprises after a period of illness, bereavement, or system access problems
- Drivers who thought a platform or employer "dealt with tax" and never registered
- Penalties sitting alongside a wider [HMRC investigation](/services/hmrc-investigations) or compliance check
