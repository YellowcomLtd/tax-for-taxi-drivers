An HMRC letter that isn't a routine reminder needs a clear head and a proper response. Compliance checks and investigations can look at your returns, your records, and how your declared income lines up with how you actually work as a taxi driver.

Tax for Taxi Drivers supports self-employed drivers in Belfast and across Northern Ireland from the first contact through to resolution — and we deal with HMRC on your behalf.

## Signs you shouldn't ignore

- Formal notices asking for records, explanations, or a meeting
- Queries about undeclared income or lifestyle vs declared profits
- Follow-ups after late or missing [Self Assessment](/services/self-assessment) returns
- Problems that started as [penalties](/services/hmrc-penalty-appeals) and have escalated
- Confusion between a **licence tax check** and a wider enquiry — if it's licence-related, see [HMRC tax checks](/services/hmrc-tax-check)

## How we support you

- Review the notice and your existing returns and accounts
- Prepare responses and supply the [income & expenditure](/services/income-expenditure-records) evidence HMRC asks for
- Correspond with HMRC so you're not stuck managing the process alone
- Rebuild accurate filings where returns were late, missing, or incomplete

**Gold** monthly plans include accountancy services for HMRC investigations. If you're not sure which plan fits, [contact us](/contact) and we'll talk it through — first chat is free and confidential.

## Local drivers, serious process

Whether you work Belfast streets, airports, or private hire across NI, the expectation from HMRC is the same: credible records and honest returns. We help you meet that without the panic.
