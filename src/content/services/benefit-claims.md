Self-employed taxi drivers are often told they "won't qualify" for help — and many leave money on the table. A proper entitlement check looks at your household, your earnings, and how HMRC and the Department for Communities (or DWP, depending on the benefit) see your income.

We help drivers in Belfast and across Northern Ireland with Universal Credit, PIP, ESA, Tax Credits and Housing Benefit — from the first check through claims and appeals.

## Who this is for

- Drivers whose hours or fares have dropped and need to know what support is available
- Households mixing self-employment with part-time PAYE or a partner's income
- Drivers told a claim was refused who want a second look
- Anyone whose [Self Assessment](/services/self-assessment) figures need to line up cleanly with a benefit claim

## What we do

- Run a **full entitlement check** for every client
- Help you claim the benefits you're entitled to
- Support appeals where a decision looks wrong
- Keep your income and expenditure figures consistent between tax and benefits — so one story doesn't contradict the other

Accurate [records](/services/income-expenditure-records) make benefit work faster and more reliable. If you're already a client, we use the same figures we prepare for HMRC.

## Northern Ireland context

Benefit administration in NI can differ from Great Britain. We work with the systems drivers here actually deal with, and we don't assume GB rules apply by default.
